# Clothes Drying Rack - Assembly Instructions

Welcome to your 3D-Printed Clothes Drying Rack! No glue or metal screws required—everything threads together by hand.

## Part Checklist
*   **Leg Segments**: Long rectangular tubes with a threaded peg on one end.
*   **Drying Rods**: Long rounded tubes with a threaded peg on one end.
*   **X-Frame Hinges**: Top and bottom hinge arms, plus the threaded pivot pin.
*   **T-Brackets**: L-shaped blocks with three threaded holes.
*   **Adapter Pins**: Short pins with threads on both sides.
*   **Caps**: Rubber-style foot caps for the base, and domed caps for the rod ends.

---

## Step 1: Build the X-Frame Hinges
1. Take one **Bottom Hinge Arm** and one **Top Hinge Arm**.
2. Align their central circular holes so they form an X shape.
3. Insert the **Pivot Pin** through both holes.
4. Twist the pin tightly to lock the hinge together. 
*(Repeat to build the second X-Frame hinge).*

---

## Step 2: Assemble the Legs
1. Take your **Leg Segments** and screw them end-to-end to build four long legs for each X-Frame. 
2. Screw the top of each completed leg into the four threaded arms of the **X-Frame Hinges**.
3. **Important:** As you tighten, align the flat rectangular sides so the leg looks smooth and continuous.

---

## Step 3: Attach Brackets and Feet
1. At the very bottom of each leg, slide on a **Foot Cap**.
2. Halfway down the leg, insert a **T-Bracket** inline between two leg segments. (This will hold the middle stability bar).
3. At the very top of each leg, screw on a **T-Bracket** facing inward.

---

## Step 4: Build the Horizontal Rods
1. Take your **Drying Rods** and screw them end-to-end to create your 3 main spanning rods, plus 1 middle stability bar.
2. At the flat end of each finished rod, screw in an **Adapter Pin** so that both ends of the rod have a protruding male thread.

---

## Step 5: Final Assembly
1. Stand both completed X-Frames upright, facing each other.
2. Screw the **Middle Stability Bar** into the midway T-Brackets to connect the two frames.
3. Screw the **Top Drying Rods** into the side holes of the top T-Brackets.
4. Finally, cap off any open upward-facing holes on the top T-brackets using the **Rod End Caps**.

**You're done! Your clothes drying rack is fully assembled and ready for laundry day.**
